# Nester Protocol: Terms of Service & Risk Disclosures

*Review by Legal Counsel & Core Contributors Prior to Mainnet Launch*

## 1. Overview & Scope

Nester is a decentralized, self-custodial savings and yield investment protocol operating on the Stellar network. By accessing, interacting with, or depositing funds into Nester Smart Vaults, you acknowledge and agree to the terms, conditions, and risk disclosures set forth below.

## 2. No FDIC or Government Insurance

**Nester is not a bank, depository institution, or regulated financial institution.** 

* **No FDIC / SIPC Protection:** Digital assets deposited into Nester Smart Vaults are **not** insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC), the Securities Investor Protection Corporation (SIPC), or any other governmental or private agency.
* **Risk of Total Loss:** In the event of protocol failure, smart contract exploits, extreme market downturns, or underlying DeFi partner insolvency, you may lose some or all of your deposited principal and earned yields.

## 3. Smart Contract & Protocol Risks

Nester utilizes automated on-chain smart contracts and adapters interfacing with third-party decentralized finance (DeFi) protocols (such as Blend, Soroswap, and lending pools):

* **Smart Contract Vulnerabilities:** Despite professional security audits, smart contracts may contain undiscovered bugs, logical flaws, vulnerabilities, or attack vectors.
* **Third-Party Protocol Risk:** Yields and deposits are allocated across multiple external protocols. A failure, hack, exploit, or de-pegging event in any underlying protocol directly impacts Nester Smart Vault positions.
* **Oracle and Network Congestion:** Network outages, high gas/transaction fees, or compromised oracle feeds on the Stellar network may delay or prevent deposits, withdrawals, and harvesting actions.

## 4. Yield Variability & Performance

* **Variable Returns:** Target APYs and projected yields displayed in the Nester interface are estimates based on historical on-chain data and current market conditions. They are **not guaranteed**.
* **Market Volatility:** Yields fluctuate dynamically based on supply, demand, reward token emissions, and macroeconomic factors. Negative yields or impermanent loss may occur.

## 5. Self-Custodial Responsibility

* You retain sole custody and responsibility for your private keys, seed phrases, and wallet security.
* Nester core contributors, developers, and DAO entities cannot recover lost private keys, reverse transactions, or freeze assets on your behalf.

## 6. Regulatory Compliance & Acknowledgement

By proceeding with a deposit on mainnet, you represent that you are legally permitted to use decentralized finance applications in your jurisdiction and that you have independently evaluated the associated technical and financial risks.
